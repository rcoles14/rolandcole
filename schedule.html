Coverage Planner

14 On / 7 Off Schedule Builder

This app creates a staggered 21-day rotation for three people: one assigned to Nevada, one assigned to Arizona, and one relief person who covers the other two during their 7-day off blocks.

Days 1-7 Relief is off. Nevada and Arizona are both on.
Days 8-14 Arizona is off. Relief covers Arizona.
Days 15-21 Nevada is off. Relief covers Nevada.

Schedule Inputs

Pick a cycle start date and names, then generate the rotation.

  • Each person follows the same 21-day cycle.
  • Each person gets 14 days on and 7 days off.
  • The relief person fills the off block for AZ and NV.
