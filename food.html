BBQ Gallery

A few cooks I am proud of.

Beef Cheek

Beef cheek smoked on an offset and finished in a tallow confit for ultimate tenderness. Shredded the leftovers for barbacoa tacos. This was my first time cooking beef cheek and it turned out amazing.

Brisket

Small brisket flat I made when my wife was out of town. She really missed out. Cooked on a pellet grill with post oak wood.

Pastrami

Brisket brined for 7 days and smoked on a pellet grill. Sliced and served on Rye with swiss cheese and russian dressing. This is a March favorite.

Beef Ribs

The mighty Dino Rib. Thick, juicy, and perfect bark. Cooked on a pellet grill.

Direct Heat Chicken

Nothing beats the crispy skin and juicy meat of chicken cooked over direct heat. This was cooked over coals with some hickory chunks.

Pulled Pork

Mexican-style pulled pork tacos with a tangy jalepeno ranch, cooked low and slow on an offset smoker. This was my first cook on an offset smoker.

Thanksgiving Turkey

A few of my thanksgiving turkeys cooked on the pellet grill. The first one was a 14lb bird and the second I decided to just do sliced breasts, similar to how traditional Texas BBQ joints do turkey.

Chili

The ward cookoff championship winning chili. It's a secret recipe ;) Texans beware, it has beans.
